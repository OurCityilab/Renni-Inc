# TOMORROW — Day 1 Run-of-Show (one page, emergency copy)
## Money in Real Life · Day 1: Your First Check Is Not Your Whole Check

**Bring:** printed case packets (1/student — print `/studio/curriculum/financial-literacy-case-packet.md`), event cards 1–8 cut apart, role cards cut apart, chart paper, markers, index cards (exit tickets), timer. Devices needed ONLY for the 145–175 block.

**Board before students arrive:**
`$15.50/hr × 21 hrs × 6 weeks = how much is YOURS?`

| Time | What | You say / do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | **Hook** | "Gut number, no calculator, write it down." Collect guesses on the board. No judgment. |
| 0:15–0:35 | **Mini-lesson** | Gross vs. take-home. Board math once: $15.50×21×6 = **$1,953** → ×0.75 ≈ **$1,465** ≈ **$244/wk**. "Nobody stole from you — but plan around the wrong number and the gap comes out of YOUR plans." |
| 0:35–0:50 | **Teams + roles** | Teams of 3–4. Deal role cards (they keep them ALL WEEK). Packet rounds 1–2. |
| 0:50–1:25 | **First-Check Plan** | Packet round 3: transportation, food, phone, helping at home (dignity, always), savings. Circulate. Challenge $0-food budgets: "Show me the Tuesday you work 1–6." |
| 1:25–1:35 | **BREAK** | |
| 1:35–1:45 | **Surprise card** | One event card per team (cards 1–8). "This is not a punishment. This is a Tuesday." |
| 1:45–2:10 | **Revise** | Rewrite the plan + one sentence: what we cut and why. |
| 2:10–2:25 | **Debrief circle** | 1) What did you think was yours that wasn't? 2) What did you protect — why that? 3) Who cut savings first? What would make you cut it last? |
| 2:25–2:55 | **STUDIO CHECKPOINT** | Devices open NOW, not before. `Studio → The Lab → Financial Literacy → Take-Home Pay`. Enter case (or real) numbers, confirm the calculator matches their math, **Save my answers**. "The case was the work — this is your receipt." |
| 2:55–3:15 | **Coach clinic** | Pick from: reading a pay stub, FICA in one sentence, why check #1 is small, check-cashing fees vs. direct deposit. |
| 3:15–3:45 | **Share-outs** | 60 sec/team: original plan → what the card broke → final plan. Score publicly: realism / essentials protected / revision quality (0–3 each). |
| 3:45–4:00 | **Exit ticket** | Index card: one sentence ("what wasn't yours?") + one number (realistic weekly take-home). Collect packets stay with students; collect exit tickets + photo each team's plan. |

**If tech fails:** entire day runs on paper; module becomes tomorrow's opener. **If time runs short:** cut share-out rounds, NEVER the surprise-card revision.

**Tone rules:** practice numbers, not family business · "helping at home" is respectable, never a burden story · feedback is "hard to picture / needs a number," never "irresponsible."

**Tonight after class:** skim exit tickets; note which teams cut savings first — Day 2's $1,300 Month lands harder when you quote their own cuts back to them.
