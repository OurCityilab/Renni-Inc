# Simulation Scoreboard & Rubric
## Money in Real Life — Financial Literacy Simulation Week

Post the scoreboard where everyone can see it. Update it publicly at the end of each day. Points reward thinking, not luck — a team that eats a brutal event card and revises well outscores a team that coasted.

## Daily team scoreboard

Score each criterion 0–3 per day. Max 24/day, 96 for the week (Friday is unscored).

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Realism** | Numbers are fantasy | Some real numbers | Mostly credible | Every number defensible; sources/estimates named |
| **Tradeoff explanation** | "Because we wanted to" | Names what was cut | Names what was cut and why | Weighs both sides out loud before choosing |
| **Protection of essentials** | Essentials cut first | Essentials mostly survive | Essentials protected | Essentials protected AND the plan says how |
| **Emergency planning** | No cushion anywhere | Cushion mentioned | Real cushion with a number | Cushion funded first and defended under pressure |
| **Credit risk awareness** | Signs anything | Spots the obvious trap | Computes true cost before deciding | Computes true cost AND names the structure that would make it safe |
| **Revision after surprise events** | Plan abandoned | Plan patched | Plan revised with reasons | Revised, reasons written, essentials intact |
| **Presentation quality** | One voice, no numbers | Clear but numberless | Clear with numbers | Clear, numbers, every teammate speaks |
| **Reflection honesty** | Blank/generic | Safe answer | Real answer | Real answer that names a change they'll make |

**Scoring notes for the coach**
- Score in front of the room for at least two criteria per day — the argument about the score IS instruction.
- Never score down for the situation on a role/event card, only for the response to it.
- Tie-breaker for the week: quality of the final My Money Plan pitch.

| Artifact | Day | Saved where |
|---|---|---|

| My Budget Defense / My Budget Plan | 2 | Paper + Budget Builder save in Studio |

| My Money Plan (capstone) | 4 | Studio — Money Plan module |
| Field Trip Money Reflection | 5 | Paper; optionally added to My Money Plan |

Quality bar per artifact: has real numbers, has one honest sentence, and (for Studio saves) exists as a saved draft. That's it — this is a floor, not a competition.

## Student-safe feedback language (enforced on peers and coach alike)

Use: "hard to picture," "needs a number," "unsupported," "too general," "what would make this a yes?"
Never: "lazy," "irresponsible," "bad with money," or anything aimed at a student's actual family situation.
