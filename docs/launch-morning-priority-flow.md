## Student workflow

1. Open your group card on the home dashboard (Launch Morning Priorities).
2. Click the section button.
3. Use the builder / table / checklist at the top of the section.
4. Copy the output.
5. Paste into Working Draft.
6. Edit it in your own words.
7. Save.
8. Tell your chief it is ready to review.

## Chief workflow

1. Open Project Navigator.
2. Check **blocked**, **overdue**, and **ready** work.
3. Review the most launch-critical sections first — finance and
   operations sections that gate TechTown go ahead of brand and BMC.
4. If a draft is generic, ask: "What would the customer or the next
   cohort need to know that this draft doesn't say yet?"
