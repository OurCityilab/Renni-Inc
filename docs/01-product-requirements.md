# Product Requirements

## Product name
Renni Command Center

## Core purpose
A lightweight production environment for the Renni Inc. Brand and Operations Bible and the TechTown pop-up shop.

## Primary outcomes
1. Keep all students and chiefs aligned in one shared system.
2. Provide seeded deliverables, templates, due dates, and definitions of done.
3. Support creation of the final Bible.
4. Support preparation for the TechTown pop-up.
5. Support the Phoenix Nest retail carry pitch.
6. Provide analysis/scenario support where it is genuinely useful.

## Locked company context
- Audience: Renaissance students and stakeholders
- Parent company: Renni Inc.
- Flagship brand: House Phoenix
- Supporting brands documented in the Bible: Lumen, Notice, Humble Oven
- Current pop-up products: beanies, sweatshirts, t-shirts, baked goods, donations
- Current posture: House Phoenix-led public launch under Renni Inc.

- Co-CEO
- Co-CEO
- COO

- Chief Strategy and Growth Officer

## Must-have modules (V1)
- Login and role-aware access
- Home dashboard
- Department dashboards
- C-suite dashboard
- Task tracker
- Read-only Gantt view
- Goal/KPI tracker
- Bible view with chapter status
- Business Model Canvas builder
- Company Structure & Continuity module
- Decision log with required “why” fields
- Pricing and break-even engine
- Donation tracker
- Phoenix Nest retail carry worksheet
- Transaction ledger for pop-up day
- Customer feedback feed
- Sherpa Lite coach
- Responsible AI Use page

## Non-negotiable design rules
- Mobile-responsive from day one
- Seed all deliverables before launch
- Approval rubric visible on every deliverable
- Do not use last-write-wins for shared strategic modules
- Approval state lives in app data, not in linked docs
- Keep real math separate from softer brand/strategy reasoning support

## Approval rubric
A deliverable can move to Approved only if it is:
1. Complete
2. Accurate
3. Reviewed by the department head
4. In the correct format/template
5. Usable by the next cohort

## Definition of done
A deliverable is only done when:
- the final version exists
- it has been internally reviewed
- it has been approved by the correct chief or Co-CEO
- it is stored in the right location
- it is usable in the pop-up, Phoenix Nest pitch, or Bible
