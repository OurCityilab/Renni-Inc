# The Lab Feature Spec

The Lab helps students translate raw experiences and ideas into polished professional artifacts.

## Core workflow

1. Student enters raw words.
2. AI asks clarifying questions.
3. Student adds detail.
4. AI generates polished options.
5. AI explains why the language improved.
6. Student chooses or edits.
7. Coach can review.
8. Final artifact is saved to portfolio.

## Module 1: Personal Brand Builder

Inputs: what they care about, problems they notice, who they want to help, what people ask them for help with, strengths, shaped experiences, goals.

Brand ingredients: purpose, passion, values, superpowers, differentiators, goals, focus word / idea / story sentence.

Outputs: three brand words, personal brand sentence, “I help ___ do ___ by using ___,” short professional summary, and coach-facing notes on areas needing detail.

## Module 2: Story Bank

Students collect experiences that can become interview answers, resume bullets, or LinkedIn content.

Story fields: situation, role/responsibility, actions taken, people involved, obstacles, numbers/results, lesson learned, skills shown.

## Module 3: STAR / TMAY Sherpa

Turns raw stories into STAR responses, natural spoken interview answers, TMAY paragraphs, resume bullets, and LinkedIn experience descriptions.

Required follow-up prompts: How many people were involved? How much money was made or saved? How many customers/users/students were affected? How long did it take? What changed because of your work? What was the before and after?

## Module 4: Resume Sherpa

Inputs: project/job/activity name, role, tasks, tools, audience/customer, outcome, metrics, skills.

Outputs for each resume item: basic bullet, stronger bullet, strongest bullet with numbers, explanation of improvement, and missing details.

## Module 5: LinkedIn Sherpa

Outputs: headline, About section, experience entries, project descriptions, skills list, profile completion checklist.

Tone should be professional, student-appropriate, and not inflated. Avoid words like visionary, seasoned, expert, and adult corporate jargon that does not match the student.

## Module 6: Pitch Builder

Generate: 3-second intro, 30-second pitch, 3-minute TMAY, worksite intro, interview version, networking version.

3-second formula: Name + identity + direction.

30-second formula: Who I am, what I care about, what I am building/learning, where I am going.

3-minute formula: opening identity, origin story, strengths and proof, current work, future goal, closing statement.

## Portfolio behavior

Final outputs should be editable, exportable, and versioned.
