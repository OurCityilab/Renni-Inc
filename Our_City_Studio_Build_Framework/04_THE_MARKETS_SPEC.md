# The Markets Feature Spec

The Markets are the financial literacy side of Our City Studio. They teach through simulation, not lectures.

## Core principle

Students learn best when they feel the consequences: rejected because of credit, approved but cash-flow poor, underpriced and lost money, overpriced and lost customers, sold out but missed profit, bought property and became a landlord, rented because they could not qualify to buy.

## Modules

### 1. Money Basics

Short interactive lessons: income, take-home pay, expenses, needs vs. wants, savings, emergency fund, budgeting.

Outputs: weekly budget, savings goal, spending reflection.

### 2. Keys & Credit

Qualification game: career/salary, credit score, savings, debt, car qualification, rent qualification, mortgage qualification.

Outputs: affordability report, credit game plan, car decision reflection, rent/home qualification summary.

### 3. Rose City Marketplace

Housing and landlord simulation. Students receive jobs/credit/savings/debt, try to qualify for cars/housing, compete for homes, rent from landlords, become landlords, set rents/deposits/approval standards, and respond to market events.

Outputs: personal money report, housing status, monthly cash flow, landlord cash flow if applicable, reflection on market dynamics.

### 4. Rose City Market Day

Goods and services marketplace. Students own a small business, set inputs/quality/capacity/price, spend a personal consumer budget, buy from classmates or NPC stores, respond to market events, calculate P&L and KPIs, and present results.

Outputs: business card, pricing sheet, P&L, KPI report, market reflection, business presentation.

### 5. Real Estate Deal Builder

Students create a back-of-the-napkin analysis, real estate sell sheet, simple pro forma, and investor summary.

Inputs: purchase price, rehab estimate, rent or resale value, taxes, insurance, financing assumptions, risks.

Outputs: deal summary, monthly cash flow, risk notes, AI explanation.
