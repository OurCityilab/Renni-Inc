# Seed Data Spec

## Career cards

Each career card: title, annual_salary, monthly_gross, career_category, education_or_training, growth_note.

Sample careers: Medical Assistant 42000, Electrician Apprentice 48000, CDL Driver 52000, Teacher 55000, Police Officer 62000, Auto Technician 50000, Marketing Coordinator 58000, Construction Project Coordinator 65000, Nurse 78000, Software Developer 95000, Real Estate Agent 60000 variable, Entrepreneur 45000 variable.

## Credit score cards

Bands: Poor 520–579, Fair 580–639, Okay 640–699, Good 700–749, Excellent 750–810.

Each band should map to car APR estimate, mortgage APR estimate, rental competitiveness, and approval notes.

## Savings cards

500, 1500, 3000, 5000, 10000, 20000.

## Debt cards

No debt 0, Student loan 250, Credit card minimum 150, Car note existing 400, Combined debt 600, Collections event monthly 100 plus credit impact.

## Property cards

Rental fields: name, neighborhood, rent, deposit, min_credit, income_multiplier, quality, scarcity.

For-sale fields: name, neighborhood, purchase_price, required_down_payment_percent, taxes_monthly, insurance_monthly, maintenance_monthly, expected_rent, resale_value, risk_rating.

## NPC stores

Budget Barn, Main Street Standard, Premium House, Convenience Corner, Trendy Pop-Up.

Fields: category, price, quality_score, inventory, satisfaction_baseline, strategy_note.

## Market events

Housing: Interest rates rise, rents increase, property taxes increase, insurance rates rise, car repair, credit score improves, missed payment, extra hours.

Market Day: Supplier cost increase, premium supplies unavailable, NPC sale, trend shift, viral boost, bad review, booth fee increase, big event increases demand.
