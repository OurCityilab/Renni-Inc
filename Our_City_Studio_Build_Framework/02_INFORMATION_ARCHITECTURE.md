# Information Architecture

## Primary navigation

Our City Studio should have four simple top-level areas.

### 1. Today

The home screen. Shows exactly what a student should do now: current mission, time estimate, status, continue button, ask AI button, ask coach button, and due date if applicable.

Example: Today's Mission: Turn one real experience into a STAR answer. Time: 25 minutes. Output: polished interview response + resume bullet.

### 2. The Lab

Professional development modules: Personal Brand Builder, Story Bank, STAR/TMAY Sherpa, Resume Sherpa, LinkedIn Sherpa, Pitch Builder, Portfolio Review.

### 3. The Markets

Financial literacy modules: Money Basics, Keys & Credit, Rose City Marketplace, Rose City Market Day, Business Finance, Real Estate Deal Builder.

### 4. My Portfolio

Student-facing library of finished work: brand sentence, intros/pitches, STAR answers, resume bullets, LinkedIn sections, budget, credit plan, money report, P&L, KPI report, sell sheet, pro forma, reflections.

## Coach/Admin navigation

Coach view should be separate from student view: Cohort Overview, Student Progress, Submissions, AI Drafts Needing Review, Simulation Sessions, Export Center, Settings.

## UX rule

Students should never land on a blank dashboard. Every student session should start with a clear next action.
