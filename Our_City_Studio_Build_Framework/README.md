# Our City Studio

**Our City Studio** is a mobile-first learning and portfolio platform for Rose City / Our City Summer Academy students.

It has two primary product areas:

1. **The Lab** — professional development, personal brand, resume, LinkedIn, interview prep, TMAY/STAR stories, and student portfolio outputs.
2. **The Markets** — financial literacy through interactive simulations, including housing/credit qualification and goods-and-services marketplace games.

The product should feel less like an LMS and more like a guided studio: students enter raw words, choices, and numbers; the platform helps them clarify, improve, calculate, practice, and export finished work.

## Product principle

> Student gives raw truth. AI asks better questions. Student adds detail. AI translates it into professional language or financial insight. Coach validates it. Finished product goes into the student portfolio.

## Build posture

Do not rebuild a dense command center. Our City Studio must be simple enough for high school students to use on mobile without a tutorial.

Prioritize mobile-first tasks, one action per screen, visible progress, AI help, coach review, portfolio exports, and simulations instead of passive lessons.

## MVP order

1. Authentication and student profiles
2. Today page
3. The Lab: worksheet flow + AI Sherpa + portfolio outputs
4. The Markets: Rose City Marketplace housing/credit simulation
5. The Markets: Rose City Market Day goods/services marketplace
6. Coach dashboard
7. Exportable portfolio reports and PDFs
