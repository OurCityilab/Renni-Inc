# Our City Studio Product Brief

## One-line description

Our City Studio is a mobile-first platform where students complete guided professional development and financial literacy experiences, with AI coaching that turns raw student inputs into polished, explainable, portfolio-ready outputs.

## Audience

Primary users are Rose City / Our City Summer Academy students. Secondary users are facilitators, coaches, worksite leads, and program administrators.

## Product areas

### The Lab: Professional Development

Students build a personal brand sentence, 3-second intro, 30-second pitch, 3-minute TMAY, STAR interview responses, resume bullets, LinkedIn headline, LinkedIn About section, worksite introduction, and student portfolio.

### The Markets: Financial Literacy

Students learn through simulations: budgeting, take-home pay, credit, debt-to-income ratio, car qualification, rent qualification, mortgage qualification, landlord decisions, housing supply/demand, goods/services marketplace, pricing, COGS, quality, competition, P&L, KPIs, and business reporting.

## What makes this different

Most financial literacy and career-readiness tools are passive: videos, quizzes, worksheets. Our City Studio is active: students make decisions, the system calculates consequences, AI asks follow-up questions, students improve their language and reasoning, and outputs become real artifacts.

## North Star

Students leave the academy with a professional self-presentation package, starter resume/LinkedIn language, interview-ready stories, a working understanding of money and markets, and a portfolio of work they can use after the program.

## Non-goals for MVP

Do not build a full LMS, complex project management views, a social network, dense student dashboards, full real-world underwriting, legal/financial advice, or permanent public student profiles.
