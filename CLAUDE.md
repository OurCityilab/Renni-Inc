# CLAUDE.md

## Project mission
Build a lightweight, modular command center that helps Renaissance students complete the Renni Inc. final project and produce a usable Brand and Operations Bible.

## Priorities
1. Make the app usable fast.
2. Keep students aligned around seeded deliverables.
3. Surface what chiefs need to push on.
4. Preserve approval logic and auditability.
5. Avoid unnecessary complexity.

## Coding guidance
- Keep components small and legible.
- Prefer straightforward Firestore queries.
- Use soft locking instead of complex collaborative editing.
- Preserve mobile usability.
- Do not introduce drag-and-drop or rich-text editor complexity in V1.

## Product truths
- This is for Renaissance students.
- House Phoenix is the flagship launch brand.
- Current pop-up products are beanies, sweatshirts, t-shirts, baked goods, and donations.
- Strategy and Growth replaces R&D.
