## Purpose
Translate customer feedback and pop-up observations into recommendations for the next cohort.

## Approval rubric
A deliverable can move to **Approved** only if it is:
1. Complete
2. Accurate
3. Reviewed by the department head
4. In the correct format / template
5. Usable by the next cohort

## Definition of done
- Final version exists
- Internally reviewed
- Approved by the correct chief or Co-CEO
- Stored in the right location
- Usable in the pop-up, Phoenix Nest pitch, or Playbook

## Sections
1. Customer segment observations
2. Evidence summary (feedback entries, sales patterns, floor observations)
3. Assumptions that held vs. broke
4. Three to five strategic priorities for next semester
5. Product and brand recommendations
6. Growth bets worth testing

## Notes for next cohort
- Pull from the Feedback module, not memory. Every recommendation must cite evidence — feedback IDs, transaction patterns, observed behavior.
- If the data is thin for a recommendation, say so rather than overstating confidence. "Weak signal" is still a useful label.
- Flag any assumption the current cohort couldn't validate so the next team knows where to look first.
