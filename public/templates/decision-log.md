## Purpose
A running log of every significant decision Renni Inc. made this semester, plus appendices for templates and evidence.

## Approval rubric
A deliverable can move to **Approved** only if it is:
1. Complete
2. Accurate
3. Reviewed by the department head
4. In the correct format / template
5. Usable by the next cohort

## Definition of done
- Final version exists
- Internally reviewed
- Approved by the correct chief or Co-CEO
- Stored in the right location
- Usable in the pop-up, Phoenix Nest pitch, or Playbook

## Sections
1. Decision log entries (one row per decision)
2. Why each decision was made (rationale)
3. Alternatives considered
4. Expected outcome vs actual outcome
5. Appendices — templates, trackers, evidence

## Decision entry fields (portal)
- Title
- Category
- Decision
- Rationale (**required why**)
- Alternatives considered
- Expected outcome
- Decided by
- Related chapter / deliverable

## Notes for next cohort
- Decisions are captured in the portal's **Decision Log** module. The `why` field is required — the portal will reject an entry without it.
- This chapter prints the log and highlights the decisions worth carrying forward. Don't rewrite the reasoning — just curate.
- If you're unsure whether something is a "decision," it probably is. Log it. You can always flag it as minor.
