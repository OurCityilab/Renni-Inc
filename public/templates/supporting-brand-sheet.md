## Purpose
One-pagers for Lumen, Notice, and Humble Oven that document each brand's reason to exist under Renni Inc.

## Approval rubric
A deliverable can move to **Approved** only if it is:
1. Complete
2. Accurate
3. Reviewed by the department head
4. In the correct format / template
5. Usable by the next cohort

## Definition of done
- Final version exists
- Internally reviewed
- Approved by the correct chief or Co-CEO
- Stored in the right location
- Usable in the pop-up, Phoenix Nest pitch, or Playbook

## Per-brand sections
1. Brand name and one-line descriptor
2. Audience
3. Relationship to House Phoenix and Renni Inc.
4. Visual cues (color / type / vibe)
5. Current status (dormant, in development, launched)
6. Notes for the next cohort

## Notes for next cohort
- One sheet per supporting brand. Keep each under a page.
- Do not re-launch a supporting brand without a C-suite decision recorded in the Decision Log — dormancy is the default for anything that isn't House Phoenix right now.
- If you invent a new supporting brand, add it here **and** update the Brand Architecture chapter.
