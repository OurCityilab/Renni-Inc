# Pitch Builder Guide — Our City Studio

You need more than one version of your story. This guide covers the three sizes,
shortest to longest. Draft each one, then run it through the Personal Brand
Builder for feedback.

## The 3-second pitch
One sentence. Who you are + what you do or care about.

> "I'm [name], a [precise word for you] who [one specific thing you do]."

Use it when: introducing yourself in a group, a hallway hello, the first line of
a DM or email.

## The 30-second pitch
Three to four sentences. Who you are, what you've done (one concrete example),
and what you're looking for.

1. Who you are and what you're about
2. One real example with evidence ("Last year I…")
3. What you're working toward or asking for

Use it when: meeting a recruiter, a networking event, the "introduce yourself"
moment in a meeting.

## The 1-minute / TMAY pitch ("Tell me about yourself")
The most common interview opener. Structure:

1. **Present** — who you are now (student, role, focus)
2. **Past** — one or two experiences that shaped you (use a STAR story)
3. **Future** — what you're aiming for and why this opportunity fits

Use it when: interviews for jobs, internships, scholarships, and admissions.

---

**Rules for every size:**
- Only claim things you have evidence for.
- Precise words beat impressive-sounding ones.
- Match your words to your audience — admissions, recruiter, employer, and
  customer each hear things differently. The Brand Builder's audience selector
  helps with this.
