# Resume Template — Standard Student / Early-Career Format

This is a standard, recruiter-ready layout for students and early-career
applicants. It is not an official template from any university or company — it's
a clean structure you can rebuild in Google Docs or Word, or use to fill in a
template your school, scholarship, or employer gives you.

---

## [Your Full Name]
[City, State] · [Phone] · [Email] · [LinkedIn or portfolio link, if you have one]

## Education
**[School Name]** — [City, State]
[Expected graduation: Month Year] · GPA: [add GPA if 3.0+]
Relevant coursework, honors, or programs: [add if any]

## Experience
**[Role or what you did]**, [Organization / brand / event] — [City, State]
[Month Year] – [Month Year]
- [Action verb] + [specific thing you did] + [skill or business function] + [who it was for] + [result, number, or lesson — use [add number] / [add result] until you have the real one]
- [Second bullet]
- [Third bullet]

**[Earlier role]**, [Organization] — [City, State]
[Month Year] – [Month Year]
- [Bullet]
- [Bullet]

## Projects & Activities
**[Project, club, team, or volunteer role]**
- [What you did and what came of it]

## Skills
[Tools, languages, software, certifications — only ones you can actually use]

---

**Tips:**
- Keep it to one page.
- Every bullet starts with a strong action verb (organized, built, tracked,
  presented, assisted, coordinated).
- Never invent numbers, titles, or awards. Use the Personal Brand Builder to turn
  your real experience into strong bullets first, then paste them in here.
