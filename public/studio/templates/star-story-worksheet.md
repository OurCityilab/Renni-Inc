# STAR Story Worksheet — Our City Studio

A STAR story is how you answer "Tell me about a time when…" questions in
interviews, applications, and scholarship essays. It has four parts. Fill in each
one about a single, real experience.

## S — Situation
What was going on? Set the scene in 1–2 sentences. Where were you, and what was
happening around you?
