# MONEY IN THE REAL WORLD
## Friday Field Trip — Money Observation Guide

**Our City / Rose City Studio — Financial Literacy Simulation Week**

All week you played the game on paper. Today you watch the real one. Everywhere you go today, money is moving — mostly in ways designed so you don't notice. Your job: notice.

Carry this guide. Fill it in during the trip or right after. Short, honest answers beat long fake ones.

---

## ON SITE — Observe

**1. Where do you see people spending money?**
List 3 spots. What are they buying, and was it planned or in-the-moment?

**2. What costs does this place/business probably have?**
Rent? Staff? Inventory? Lights? Insurance? List everything you can think of — then circle the one you'd have never thought of before this week.

**3. How does it make money?**
What's the actual product? Is the money in the thing itself, the volume, the location, or something you can't see from the outside?

**4. What hidden costs do most people miss?**
Think like Day 4 move-in math: what does it cost just to EXIST here before a single customer walks in?

**5. What job or business opportunity do you see?**
Something they need done, something missing, something you could do better. Be specific.

**6. If you worked here, sold here, or came here every week — what would YOU need to budget for?**
Transportation? Food? Gear? Fees? Give real numbers where you can.
